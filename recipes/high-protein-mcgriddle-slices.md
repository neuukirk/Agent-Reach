# High-Protein McGriddle Slices

**Source:** [@ryanrubiotrains on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZNi-jkBDzJ/)

A high-protein, baked take on the McGriddle, sliced into servings.

## Nutrition (per serving, makes 6)
- 228 calories
- 22 g protein
- 26 g carbs
- 3 g fat

## Ingredients
- 1 cup Bob's Red Mill 1:1 Baking Flour
- 8 oz (½ lb) 96/4 lean ground beef
- 1 cup fat-free cheddar cheese
- ½ cup 2% cottage cheese
- 2 large eggs
- ¾ cup fat-free milk
- ⅓ cup sugar-free maple syrup (plus 20–25 g for the beef)
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- Salt & pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Cook the ground beef and season with salt, pepper, and 20–25 g of the sugar-free maple syrup.
2. Combine the cooked beef with the remaining ingredients in a mixing bowl until fully blended.
3. Line an 8x8 baking dish with parchment paper. Pour in the mixture and spread evenly. Bake for 35–45 minutes.
4. Let cool slightly, then slice into 6 servings.

## Reheating
- **Refrigerated:** ~1 minute in the microwave.
- **Frozen:** Start with 2 minutes, adding time as needed.

# Sheet Pan Crunchwraps

**Source:** [@cookingwithcaitlynskitchen on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXIZAvcjtIT/)

## Ingredients
- 7–8 burrito-size tortillas
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- ¼ cup water
- 1 cup shredded Colby Jack cheese
- 6 tostada shells
- 1 jar cheese sauce
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 tbsp butter, melted

### Creamy Taco Dip
- ⅓ cup mayo
- ⅓ cup sour cream
- 1 tsp taco seasoning
- 2 tbsp mild taco sauce

## Instructions
1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat; drain. Add the taco seasoning and water; simmer until thickened.
2. Preheat oven to 400°F and grease a 9x13 sheet pan.
3. Arrange 6 tortillas around the pan, overlapping edges to cover the bottom.
4. Layer the taco meat, cheese sauce, tostada shells, sour cream, lettuce, tomato, and shredded cheese.
5. Place 1–2 tortillas in the center; fold the overhanging tortillas inward to enclose everything.
6. Brush the top with melted butter. Place another sheet pan on top. Bake 15 minutes, remove the top pan, then bake 10–15 minutes more until golden.
7. Cool slightly, then cut into 6 crunchwraps.
8. For the dip, mix the mayo, sour cream, taco seasoning, and mild sauce until smooth.

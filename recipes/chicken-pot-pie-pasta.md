# One-Pot Chicken Pot Pie Pasta

**Inspired by:** [@cookingwithkatiecross on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DARtr56uljw/) (reel had no written recipe)
**Recipe source:** [jocooks.com](https://www.jocooks.com/recipes/one-pot-creamy-chicken-pot-pie-pasta/) — a web stand-in for the same dish.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 stalk celery, chopped
- 1 medium carrot, chopped
- 6 skinless, boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- ½ tsp salt (or to taste)
- ¼ tsp pepper (or to taste)
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp fresh rosemary, chopped (or ½ tsp dried)
- ½ cup white wine
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 12 oz uncooked short pasta
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions
1. Melt the butter in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion, celery, and carrot; cook ~5 minutes until softened.
2. Add the chicken and season with salt and pepper. Cook 5–7 minutes until no longer pink.
3. Sprinkle the flour over and stir, cooking 1 minute. Add the rosemary, white wine, broth, and pasta. Stir to combine and adjust seasoning.
4. Cook 15 minutes over medium-low, stirring occasionally, then add the peas. Cook another 5–10 minutes until the pasta is done.
5. Garnish with parsley and serve.

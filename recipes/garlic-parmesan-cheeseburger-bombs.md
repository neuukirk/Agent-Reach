# Garlic Parmesan Cheeseburger Bombs

**Source:** [@ashieeeats on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZIhAY6ponx/)

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- Minced garlic
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Optional: caramelized onions, bacon
- Mild cheddar cheese (large cubes)
- Shredded mozzarella

### Assembly
- 1 can Pillsbury original flaky layers biscuit dough
- 1 egg, whisked (egg wash)
- Parmesan cheese, fresh grated

### Garlic Butter Topping
- 3 tbsp salted butter, melted
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- Dried or fresh parsley

## Instructions
1. Brown the ground beef over medium heat, then mix in the minced garlic and seasonings. Set aside.
2. Preheat oven to 375°F.
3. Stretch each biscuit into a 5-inch circle. Add beef, mozzarella, and a cheese cube in the center.
4. Fold the dough around the filling, pinch the edges to seal, and shape into a tight ball. Rest 5 minutes.
5. Place seam-side down on parchment. Brush with egg wash and sprinkle with fresh parmesan.
6. Bake 15–18 minutes at 375°F until golden.
7. Brush with the garlic butter, add more parmesan, and serve immediately.

# Garlic Parmesan Chicken Strips

**Source:** [@burnt_pellet_bbq on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C121ADrudPP/)

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- Chicken breast, cut into strips
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp salt
- Citrus Fresno hot sauce

### Flour Dredge
- 3 cups flour
- ½ cup cornstarch
- ½ tbsp garlic powder
- ½ tbsp onion powder
- ½ tbsp salt
- ½ tbsp pepper

### Garlic Parmesan Sauce
- ½ cup mayo
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- ¼ cup grated parmesan
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 head roasted garlic
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper
- ½ tsp red pepper
- ¼ tsp dried basil
- ¼ tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp sugar

## Instructions
1. Marinate the chicken strips in buttermilk mixed with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, and hot sauce for several hours.
2. Combine the flour, cornstarch, and seasonings for the dredge.
3. Coat each marinated strip thoroughly in the flour mixture.
4. Deep fry at 350°F for 5 minutes until golden and the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
5. Blend the sauce ingredients together.
6. Coat the fried strips with the garlic parmesan sauce.
7. Dip in hot sauce before serving.

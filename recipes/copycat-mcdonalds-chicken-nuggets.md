# Copycat McDonald's Chicken Nuggets

**Inspired by:** [@piecookwithme on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DV45nwUE_ap/) (reel had no written recipe)
**Recipe source:** [imhungryforthat.com](https://imhungryforthat.com/mcdonalds-chicken-nuggets-recipe/) — a web stand-in since the original wasn't posted.

> 💡 The IG creator used a mix of chicken tenderloins and thighs instead of all breast for juicier nuggets — you can grind your own blend in place of the ground chicken below.

**Yield:** ~16 nuggets

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground chicken
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ cup cornstarch
- ¾ cup water
- ¾ tbsp onion powder
- ¾ tbsp garlic powder
- ½ tbsp paprika
- ¾ tsp pepper
- ½ cup flour
- Oil, for frying

## Instructions
1. Mix the ground chicken, egg, and salt until evenly combined and slightly sticky.
2. Scoop tablespoon-sized portions and form into nugget shapes. Roll each thoroughly in cornstarch, coating all sides. Set aside on a tray.
3. Whisk together the flour, water, and seasonings (onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, pepper) until smooth — like pancake batter.
4. Dip each cornstarch-coated nugget into the batter until fully covered; let excess drip off.
5. **First fry:** Heat oil to 350°F. Fry in small batches ~3 minutes until they float and turn lightly golden. Drain on paper towels.
6. **Second fry:** Return to 350–360°F oil for ~2 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
7. Drain on a wire rack, rest briefly, and serve with your favorite dipping sauce.

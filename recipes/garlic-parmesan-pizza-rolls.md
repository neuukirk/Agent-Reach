# Garlic Parmesan Pizza Rolls

**Inspired by:** [@theonlyiamkayy on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C-dX8hBxuLq/) (Totino's × Hidden Valley brand reel, no written recipe)
**Recipe source:** [saltysidedish.com](https://www.saltysidedish.com/garlic-parmesan-pizza-rolls/) — a web stand-in for the same viral snack.

## Ingredients
- 18–20 frozen pizza rolls (keep frozen)
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- ¼ cup melted butter
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- Dried basil, to taste
- Grated parmesan cheese, generous amount
- Ranch dressing, for dipping (optional)

## Instructions
1. Pour enough vegetable oil into a saucepan to fully submerge the pizza rolls. Heat over medium-high.
2. Deep fry the pizza rolls for 3 minutes.
3. Remove carefully with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
4. Transfer to a large bowl.
5. In a separate bowl, combine the melted butter and minced garlic.
6. Drizzle the garlic butter over the rolls and stir to coat all sides.
7. Generously coat with grated parmesan and sprinkle with dried basil.
8. Toss once more and serve immediately while hot, with ranch or pizza sauce for dipping.

> **Air fryer alternative:** Air fry at 390°F for 4 minutes, flip and brush with the garlic butter mixture, then air fry another 4 minutes before tossing in parmesan.

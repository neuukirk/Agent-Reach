# Taco Crunch Supreme Wrap

**Source:** [@iramsfoodstory on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DChm_T0PqIu/)

## Ingredients

### Taco Meat
- 2 lbs ground beef
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 white onion, diced
- 1 tbsp freshly minced garlic
- 1 jalapeño, diced
- 2 tbsp oil for cooking
- ½ cup water

### Assembly
- 10 large tortillas
- 10 tostada corn tortillas (or 1 cup nacho chips)
- 10 mini tortillas
- 1 cup lettuce, sliced
- 1 cup tomato, diced
- 1 cup red onion, diced
- ½ cup sour cream
- 1 cup guacamole (optional)
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 cup cheddar cheese, shredded
- 1 cup Monterey Jack cheese, shredded
- Oil for cooking

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high; add diced onion and minced garlic, cooking until onion is translucent.
2. Add ground beef, breaking it apart until browned.
3. Mix in garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, cumin, black pepper, jalapeño, and tomato paste. Pour in the water.
4. Stir and simmer 8–10 minutes until the flavors combine and the meat is cooked through.
5. Lay a large tortilla flat; spoon taco meat onto it. Layer with tostada/chips, lettuce, tomato, red onion, sour cream, guacamole, salsa, and both cheeses.
6. Place a mini tortilla on top of the filling.
7. Fold the large tortilla edges toward the center, creating pleats.
8. Invert the wrap so the pleats are on the bottom, securing the filling.
9. Heat oil in a skillet over medium. Cook seam-side down for 3 minutes until golden.
10. Flip and cook the other side for 3 minutes. Repeat with remaining wraps.

**Tips:** Fold gently to avoid tearing; press lightly for even browning; serve immediately.

# Philly Cheesesteak Pasta

**Source:** [@raziyyz on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C1sFNQIuU29/)

**Servings:** ~2
**Nutrition (per serving):** ~496 cal | 44.5 g protein | 46 g carbs | 15.5 g fat

## Ingredients

### Pasta
- Water (for boiling)
- Salt
- 1¼ cups pasta, any shape (~100 g)

### Beef Mix
- 1 tsp olive oil or cooking spray
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, chopped
- 1 cup sliced mushrooms
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 cup lean ground beef or sirloin (94% lean preferred)
- 1 tsp each: salt, pepper, paprika, chili powder
- ¼ cup light cream cheese
- ⅓ cup fat-free plain Greek yogurt
- ⅓ cup light mozzarella cheese
- ½ cup pasta water

### Optional Toppings
- Cilantro and chili flakes

## Instructions
1. Boil salted water and cook the pasta according to package directions.
2. Heat oil in a skillet; sauté the onion, bell pepper, and mushrooms until softened.
3. Add the garlic, then brown the ground beef; season with salt, pepper, paprika, and chili powder.
4. Lower the heat; stir in the cream cheese, Greek yogurt, and mozzarella until melted.
5. Add pasta water to reach the desired sauce consistency.
6. Combine the cooked pasta with the beef mixture; garnish with cilantro and chili flakes if desired.

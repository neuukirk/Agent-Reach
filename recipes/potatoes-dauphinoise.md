# Potatoes Dauphinoise (Au Gratin)

**Inspired by:** [@cocolarkincooks on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DPFCqsLEux_/) (reel had no written recipe)
**Recipe source:** [RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/potatoes-au-gratin/) — a web stand-in since the original wasn't posted.

> 💡 For those crispy edges the IG reel highlighted, finish uncovered under the broiler for a few minutes at the end.

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups full-fat cream
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 30 g (2 tbsp) unsalted butter, melted
- 1.25 kg (2.5 lb) starchy potatoes (Russet/Maris Piper)
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- 2½ cups grated Gruyère (or cheddar/colby/havarti)
- 2 tsp fresh thyme leaves

## Instructions
1. Combine the melted butter, cream, and minced garlic in a jug.
2. Preheat the oven to 180°C/350°F.
3. Peel and slice the potatoes to 3 mm thick (a mandoline helps).
4. **First layer:** Spread one-third of the potatoes in a baking dish. Pour over one-third of the cream mixture, season with salt, pepper, and thyme, and sprinkle ¾ cup cheese.
5. Repeat for two more layers, omitting cheese on the final layer.
6. Cover with foil and bake 1 hour 15 minutes, until the center potatoes are tender.
7. Remove the foil, top with the remaining cheese, and bake 10–15 minutes until golden and bubbly (broil briefly for extra-crispy edges).
8. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

# Dumpling Lasagna

**Source:** [@platedbyyuri on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZz6SGCQVCh/)

A viral steamed "lasagna" built from layers of dumpling wrappers and seasoned pork.

## Ingredients

### Dumpling Filling
- 1 lb (450 g) ground pork
- ½ cup finely chopped onion
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 3 green onions, chopped
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

### Layers
- 25–30 dumpling wrappers
- ½ cup water or chicken stock

### Sauce
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 3–4 tbsp chili oil

### Toppings
- 2 tbsp green onions, chopped
- Chili oil

## Instructions
1. Combine ground pork, onion, garlic, ginger, green onions, both soy sauces, sesame oil, salt, and pepper. Mix until sticky.
2. Spread a thin meat layer in a heatproof dish, then add a layer of dumpling wrappers.
3. Alternate layers: meat → wrappers → meat → wrappers → meat → wrappers.
4. Pour the water or chicken stock evenly over the top.
5. Steam in a steamer over medium heat for 25–30 minutes, until the pork is fully cooked.
6. Let rest briefly after removing from the steamer.
7. Mix the soy sauce, rice vinegar, and chili oil; pour generously over the dish.
8. Finish with chili oil and chopped green onions.

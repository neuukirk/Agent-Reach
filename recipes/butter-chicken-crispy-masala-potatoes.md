# High-Protein Butter Chicken & Crispy Masala Potatoes

**Source:** [@jalalsamfit on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DBE3m6Go794/)

**Nutrition (per serving, makes 4):** 592 cal | 52 g protein | 51 g carbs | 20 g fat

## Ingredients

### Chicken Marinade
- 800 g boneless skinless chicken thighs, cubed
- 1 tbsp garlic paste
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 1.5 tsp garam masala
- 1.5 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 80–100 g low-fat yogurt
- Juice of 1 whole lemon

### Crispy Masala Potatoes
- 800 g white potatoes, cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp olive oil (optional)

### Butter Chicken Sauce
- 40 g light butter
- 150 g sliced white onion
- ½ tsp each: salt, garam masala, paprika, turmeric
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 60 g raw cashew nuts
- 600 g canned or fresh chopped tomatoes
- 100 g low-fat yogurt
- 60 ml light evaporated milk

### White Sauce
- 50 g low-fat yogurt
- 20–25 ml milk

## Instructions
1. **Marinate chicken:** Combine cubed thighs with garlic paste, salt, garam masala, paprika, turmeric, chili powder, yogurt, and lemon juice. Marinate at least 30 minutes (or overnight).
2. **Cook chicken:** Air fry at 200°C/400°F for 18–20 minutes, or pan-fry 3–4 minutes per side over medium heat.
3. **Potatoes:** Toss cubed potatoes with salt, paprika, garam masala, garlic powder, and optional olive oil. Air fry at 200°C/400°F for 20 minutes.
4. **Sauce base:** Sauté onions in butter ~5 minutes until softened. Add garlic paste and cashews, mix well.
5. **Build sauce:** Add tomatoes, cover, and simmer on low 6–8 minutes until the tomatoes break down. Cool before blending.
6. **Finish:** Blend the sauce with yogurt and evaporated milk. Combine with the cooked chicken.
7. **Serve:** Mix the white sauce ingredients for a drizzle. Plate the chicken with the crispy potatoes.

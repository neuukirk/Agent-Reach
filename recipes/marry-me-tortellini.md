# Marry Me Tortellini

**Source:** [@grilledcheesesocial on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C6cdb1cuqC_/)

One-pot meal, ready in under 20 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes in oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 pinch red pepper flakes
- 2 tbsp flour
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 20 oz cheese tortellini (5 cups)
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup parmigiano

## Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a heavy-bottomed pot; add garlic, sun-dried tomatoes with their oil, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes. Cook 3–4 minutes until fragrant.
2. Stir in the flour and cook 3 minutes to create a roux base.
3. Add the vegetable broth and bring to a boil. Let thicken 3–5 minutes, then add the tortellini with a pinch of salt.
4. Turn off the heat. Stir in the heavy cream, parmigiano, and spinach until the spinach wilts.
5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

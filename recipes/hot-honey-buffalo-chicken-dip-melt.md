# Hot Honey Buffalo Chicken Dip Melt

**Source:** [@thenaughtyfork on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C4MOz5VxJhI/)

> Full method sourced from the creator's own site: [thenaughtyfork.com](https://www.thenaughtyfork.com/all-recipes/hot-honey-buffalo-chicken-melt). It's a grilled melt (sandwich), not a baked dip. Serves 2 sandwiches.

## Ingredients

### Buffalo Chicken Dip
- 2 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
- 4 oz cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup ranch dressing (or blue cheese dressing)
- ½ cup buffalo sauce (Frank's RedHot)
- ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
- ½ cup shredded mozzarella cheese

### Assembly
- 4 slices sourdough bread
- 2 tbsp butter, softened
- ½ cup extra shredded cheddar cheese
- 4 slices applewood smoked bacon, cooked and crumbled
- ¼ cup crispy jalapeños
- ¼ cup crumbled blue cheese
- Hot honey, for drizzling

## Instructions
1. **Prepare the dip:** Combine the chicken, cream cheese, ranch, buffalo sauce, cheddar, and mozzarella in a bowl. Mix until creamy.
2. **Build sandwiches:** Butter one side of each bread slice. Spread the buffalo chicken dip on the unbuttered side of one slice. Layer with cheddar, bacon, jalapeños, and blue cheese, then drizzle with hot honey. Top with a second slice, buttered side facing out.
3. **Cook:** Heat a skillet over medium-low. Cook each sandwich 3–4 minutes per side, pressing gently, until golden and the cheese melts.
4. **Serve:** Drizzle with extra hot honey, cut in half, and serve immediately.

## Ingredients
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 2 cups shredded chicken
- ½ cup red hot sauce
- ½ cup ranch dressing
- ½ cup cheddar cheese
- Crispy jalapeños
- Applewood smoked bacon
- Blue cheese crumbles
- Hot honey drizzle

## Instructions
1. Mix together the cream cheese, shredded chicken, hot sauce, ranch, and cheddar to make the buffalo chicken dip.
2. _Remaining assembly, bake temperature, and melt steps not in the caption — see the video._ Typically: spread the dip, top with bacon, crispy jalapeños, and blue cheese, bake until bubbly, then finish with a hot honey drizzle.

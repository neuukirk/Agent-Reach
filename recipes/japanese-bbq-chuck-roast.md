# Japanese Barbecue Chuck Roast

**Inspired by:** [@violetcooksthings on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXsZah1EXd1/) (reel was DM-only — "comment 'ROAST'" — with no written recipe)
**Recipe source:** [Borrowed Bites](https://borrowedbites.com/japanese-barbecue-chuck-roast-recipe/) — a web stand-in for the same dish.

## Ingredients
- 3–4 lb chuck roast
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 2 tbsp oil (olive or avocado)
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1–2 cups Japanese-style BBQ sauce

## Instructions
1. Combine the seasonings and distribute evenly across both sides of the roast.
2. Heat the oil in a 12-inch skillet over high heat. Sear the roast 4–5 minutes per side until golden brown.
3. Transfer the seared meat to a 6-quart slow cooker (or Dutch oven).
4. Pour the broth into the hot skillet and simmer 60 seconds, scraping up the browned bits. Pour over the meat.
5. Cook on low 8–10 hours (or high 4–4.5 hours) until the meat falls apart easily. (Dutch oven: braise covered at 325°F for ~3½ hours.)
6. Remove the meat; discard large fat chunks and the cooking liquid.
7. Shred the meat into bite-sized pieces and return it to the pot.
8. Add 1–2 cups Japanese BBQ sauce, stir, and cook on low 15–30 minutes to warm through.
9. Serve over rice with your sides of choice.

🍳 Broccoli Cheddar Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 yellow onion
  • 1 lb broccoli (before trimming stalk)
  • 2 carrots
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 6 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded

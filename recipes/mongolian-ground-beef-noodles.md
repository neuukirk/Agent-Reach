# Mongolian Ground Beef Noodles

**Inspired by:** [@hermanathome on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZfxXfTA0v6/) (reel was DM-only — "comment 'recipe'" — with no written recipe)
**Recipe source:** [Table for Two](https://www.tablefortwoblog.com/mongolian-ground-beef-noodles/) — a web stand-in for the same dish.

**Servings:** 4 · **Total time:** ~20 min

## Ingredients

### Main
- 10 oz (284 g) rice noodles
- 1 lb (454 g) ground beef, 80/20
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, finely minced
- 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
- Green onions, sliced (garnish)
- White sesame seeds (garnish)

### Sauce
- ½ cup (118 ml) low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- ½ cup (118 ml) water
- ¼ cup (55 g) brown sugar
- 3 tbsp cornstarch

## Instructions
1. Combine all the sauce ingredients in a bowl and whisk together; set aside.
2. Cook the rice noodles according to package directions; set aside.
3. Heat oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef, breaking it into smaller pieces, and cook until nearly no pink remains.
4. Add the minced ginger and garlic. Cook 2–3 minutes until fragrant.
5. Pour the sauce over the beef. Simmer gently 3–4 minutes until thickened.
6. Add the cooked noodles and toss thoroughly to coat.
7. Serve, garnished with sliced green onions and sesame seeds.

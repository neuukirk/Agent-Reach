# Chilli Oil Chicken Dumplings

**Source:** [@foodieholly on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C3xuUX0NRui/)

## Ingredients
- 250 g chicken mince
- 1 carrot, grated
- 3 spring onions
- Handful chopped coriander
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- Dumpling wrappers
- 2–3 tbsp chilli oil (adjust to taste)
- Extra spring onions for topping

## Instructions
1. Combine all the dumpling filling ingredients in a bowl and mix thoroughly.
2. Scoop 1 tsp filling onto each wrapper, wet the edges, fold in half, then wet and fold the corners.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a medium-high pan; add the dumplings.
4. Pour in 150 ml water and cover with a lid.
5. Steam for 5 minutes until the water evaporates.
6. Remove the lid and cook 2–3 minutes until the bottoms crisp.
7. Serve with chilli oil and a spring onion garnish.

# Korean Gochujang Mac and Cheese

**Inspired by:** [@dandumbrell on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DE4dlZMPOUM/) (reel pointed to "full recipe on my page")
**Recipe source:** [eatwithcarmen.com](https://www.eatwithcarmen.com/gochujang-mac-and-cheese/) — a web stand-in for the same dish.

## Ingredients

### Pasta & Cheese Sauce
- 300 g macaroni
- 4 tbsp salted butter
- 4 tbsp flour
- 2 cups whole milk
- 1 cup cheddar cheese
- 1 cup white cheddar cheese
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp crème fraîche
- Pinch of salt and white pepper

### Panko Topping
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 2 tbsp salted butter
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tbsp minced parsley

## Instructions
1. Bring salted water to a boil; cook the macaroni 2 minutes short of package directions. Drain, reserving ~1 cup pasta water.
2. Melt 4 tbsp butter over medium heat. Add the flour and whisk 2 minutes to form a roux.
3. Stir in the gochujang, then gradually whisk in the milk to avoid lumps; cook until thickened.
4. Add the crème fraîche and stir. Reduce heat to low and add both cheeses, stirring until smooth. Season with salt and white pepper.
5. Add the cooked pasta and stir; loosen with reserved pasta water as needed.
6. **Topping:** Heat the oil and 2 tbsp butter in a skillet over medium. Sauté the garlic 1 minute, add panko, and stir until golden. Off heat, stir in parsley.
7. Preheat oven to 350°F. Transfer the mac to an oven-safe dish, top with the panko, and bake 15 minutes until golden and bubbling.

# Orange Pepper Chicken

**Source:** [@ryanmichaelcarter on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DITsSIqp91A/)

> Note: the full orange pepper sauce ingredient list is on the creator's blog (CasitaMade.com); the caption only listed the marinade in full.

## Ingredients

### Marinade
- 2 lbs boneless chicken thighs
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 orange, juiced
- ½ cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ¼ cup olive oil

### Orange Pepper Sauce
- Crushed peppercorns
- Garlic
- Olive oil
- Orange zest
- Salt

## Instructions
1. **Marinate:** Combine the marinade ingredients in a bowl or bag, coat the chicken thoroughly, and refrigerate 2–4 hours (minimum 30 minutes).
2. **Cook:** Grill over medium heat 12–15 minutes, flipping occasionally. Alternatively, air fry at 400°F for 25 minutes, or oven-bake at 400°F for ~30 minutes (flipping halfway). Target internal temperature: 170°F.
3. **Sauce:** Heat a skillet over medium. Sauté crushed peppercorns and garlic in olive oil until the garlic browns slightly, then reduce to low. Add remaining sauce ingredients quickly to prevent burning. Simmer briefly until thickened, remove from heat, add orange zest, and adjust salt.
4. Coat the chicken in the sauce and serve.

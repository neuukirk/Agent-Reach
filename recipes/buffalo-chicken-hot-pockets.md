# High-Protein Buffalo Chicken Hot Pockets

**Source:** [@fairfiteats on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZk6CVEIAJo/)

> ⚠️ **Partial:** The caption listed full ingredients plus freeze/reheat notes, but not the assembly/bake steps (shown in the video). Check the reel for the method.

**Yield:** 10 hot pockets
**Macros (per serving):** 425 cal | 50 g protein | 44 g carbs | 4 g fat

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 500 g self-rising flour
- 520 g 0% fat Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp (15 g) garlic salt
- 1 tbsp (8 g) Italian seasoning

### Buffalo Chicken Filling
- 40 oz (1135 g) chicken breast
- 0.5 tbsp (7 g) garlic salt
- 0.5 tbsp (3 g) smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp (8 g) ranch seasoning
- 0.5 tbsp (3 g) black pepper
- 4 tbsp (60 g) tomato salsa
- 1 cup (100 g) diced white onions
- 0.5 cup (120 g) buffalo sauce
- 3.5 cups (392 g) fat-free mozzarella
- 10 g fresh chives

### High-Protein Buffalo Sauce
- 1.5 cups (335 g) cottage cheese
- 2 oz (56 g) ⅓-fat cream cheese
- 0.5 tbsp (7 g) garlic salt
- 0.5 tbsp (3 g) black pepper
- 0.5 tbsp (3 g) onion powder
- 0.5 cup (120 g) buffalo sauce
- 0.25 cup (60 g) fat-free milk

## Freezing & Reheating
- **Freeze:** Wrap in foil or place in airtight freezer bags; store up to 1 month.
- **Reheat:** Wrap a frozen pocket in a wet paper towel, microwave 3–4 minutes, then optionally toast in a pan or air fryer for crispiness.

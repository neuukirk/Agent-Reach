# Chicken Pickle Skewers

**Source:** [@allrecipes on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DIuet_7s2NX/)

## Ingredients

### Skewers
- 1 (24 oz) jar dill pickle chips
- 1 tbsp packed light brown sugar
- 1¾ tsp kosher salt (divided)
- 1 lb skinless, boneless chicken thighs, trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
- 4 tsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp ranch dressing mix

### Sauce
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter (divided)
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- ¼ cup reduced-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tbsp ranch dressing mix
- 2 tsp fresh dill, chopped
- ⅛ tsp kosher salt

## Instructions
1. Transfer the pickles to a bowl, reserving the juice in the jar.
2. Add brown sugar and 1 tsp salt to the pickle juice; whisk until dissolved.
3. Submerge the chicken in the brine; refrigerate 3–12 hours.
4. Preheat oven to 400°F; line a baking sheet with foil.
5. Thread chicken and pickle chips alternately onto skewers (chicken–pickle–chicken–pickle–chicken).
6. Pat dry; brush with oil and season with ranch mix and the remaining salt.
7. Grill over medium-high heat until marked, about 1 minute per side.
8. Bake until internal temperature reaches 165°F, 7–12 minutes.
9. For the sauce, melt butter with garlic, broth, ranch mix, dill, and salt; spoon over the skewers.

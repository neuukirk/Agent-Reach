# High-Protein Chilli Peanut Chicken Noodles

**Source:** [@jalalsamfit on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DBuMVU9oPQv/)

**Nutrition (per serving, makes 4):** 597 cal | 54 g protein | 48 g carbs | 19 g fat

## Ingredients

### Marinated Chicken
- 800 g raw chicken breasts, cubed
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp garlic paste
- 1 tbsp ginger paste
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1.5 tsp onion powder
- 25 g sriracha
- 30 ml soy sauce (15 ml light + 15 ml dark)
- 20 g light butter (for cooking)
- Handful chopped coriander

### Chilli Peanut Sauce & Noodles
- 100 g natural peanut butter (no palm oil)
- 75 g soy sauce (45 g light + 30 g dark)
- 50 g sriracha
- 30 g rice vinegar
- 1 tsp chilli flakes (optional)
- 125–150 ml hot noodle water
- 250 g uncooked medium egg noodles (≈570 g cooked)
- ½ cup chopped green onion
- Handful chopped coriander
- Handful sesame seeds

## Instructions
1. Marinate the cubed chicken at least 30 minutes (overnight preferred).
2. Cook the chicken 3–4 minutes per side over medium heat until golden brown.
3. Boil the noodles 4–5 minutes; drain and rinse under cold water to stop cooking.
4. Warm the peanut sauce over low heat with the reserved noodle water until silky smooth.
5. Combine the noodles with the sauce; top with green onion, coriander, and sesame seeds.
6. To reheat: warm in a pan 5–10 minutes on low with a splash of added water.

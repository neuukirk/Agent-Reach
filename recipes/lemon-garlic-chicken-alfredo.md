# Lemon Garlic Chicken Alfredo

**Source:** [@iramsfoodstory on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZMwQLgNJrO/)

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 1 chicken breast, halved lengthwise
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp oil (for marinating)
- 2 tbsp oil (for cooking)

### Sauce
- 1½ tbsp all-purpose flour
- 5 tbsp butter
- 1½ cups unsweetened heavy whipping cream, room temperature
- Salt, to taste
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- ¾ cup freshly grated parmesan
- 1 tbsp lemon zest
- 2 tsp lemon juice

### To Assemble
- 9 oz fettuccine
- 1 cup pasta water

## Instructions
1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika. Coat with 1 tbsp oil and marinate 30 minutes.
2. Boil salted water and cook the fettuccine al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water; drain.
3. Cook the marinated chicken 4–5 minutes per side over medium-high heat in oil. Remove from the pan.
4. Add the butter to the pan; melt and whisk in the flour for 30–40 seconds without browning.
5. Pour in the cream and mix. Add salt, garlic powder, black pepper, Italian seasoning, and parsley.
6. Simmer 3–4 minutes, stirring, until slightly thickened.
7. Reduce heat to low; stir in the parmesan until melted and smooth.
8. Add the lemon zest and juice; adjust seasoning.
9. Toss the pasta with the sauce, adding ¼ cup reserved pasta water at a time until silky.
10. Slice the chicken, add, and toss. Simmer 1–2 minutes on low.
11. Garnish with extra parmesan, parsley, and black pepper.

# High-Protein Buffalo Chicken Potato Bowls

**Source:** [@jalalsamfit on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C7UjkH2I__o/)

**Nutrition (per serving, makes 4):** 515 cal | 44 g protein | 46 g carbs | 17 g fat

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 750 g raw chicken breast, cubed
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 2 tsp parsley
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1.5 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 100 g buffalo hot sauce (for cooking)
- 30 g light butter (for cooking)

### Crispy Potatoes
- 800 g raw white potatoes, cubed
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 1.5 tsp smoked paprika
- 1.5 tsp garlic powder
- 1.5 tsp parsley
- 1.5 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp olive oil or cooking spray (optional)

### Tangy Buffalo Sauce
- 150 g buffalo hot sauce (Frank's Red Hot)
- 100 g low-fat yogurt
- 100 g light mayonnaise
- 25 g honey

### Fresh Side Salad
- 150 g fresh tomatoes, chopped
- 100 g red onion, finely chopped
- 100 g cucumber, deseeded and chopped
- 50 g green onion/scallion, chopped
- 25 g fresh parsley, chopped
- ½ tsp each: salt, black pepper, garlic powder, cumin
- ½ lemon, juiced

## Instructions
1. Cube the chicken and season thoroughly with salt, parsley, garlic powder, cumin, and smoked paprika.
2. Cube the potatoes and toss with salt, smoked paprika, garlic powder, parsley, and cumin. Optionally drizzle with olive oil or cooking spray.
3. Cook the chicken in light butter or cooking spray until golden and charred. Lower heat, add 100 g buffalo sauce to the pan, and coat evenly. (Air fry or roast the potatoes until crispy.)
4. Make the buffalo sauce by combining Frank's Red Hot, low-fat yogurt, light mayo, and honey. Store separately.
5. Combine the salad ingredients with the seasonings and lemon juice. Store separately to keep fresh.
6. To reheat, warm chicken and potatoes in an air fryer at 200°C for 5–6 minutes until the potatoes re-crisp.
7. Assemble bowls with chicken, potatoes, salad, and buffalo sauce.

# Garlic Butter Crispy Soy Honey Chicken Sandwich

**Source:** [@donaldo_cooks on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DFNmGL7JCut/)

**Nutrition:** ~551 calories, 27 g protein per serving

## Ingredients

### Air Fryer Chicken
- 4 oz chicken breast
- Flour
- Egg
- Crushed corn flakes

### Garlic Butter Soy Sauce
- Light butter
- Minced garlic
- Light soy sauce
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- Honey
- ¼ cup water

### Sriracha Sour Cream
- Light sour cream
- Sriracha

### Other Components
- Ciabatta bread
- 1 whole cucumber
- Red onion slices
- Avocado oil
- Hot sauce
- Salt and pepper
- Grated garlic
- Parsley
- Red pepper flakes

## Instructions
1. Dredge chicken in flour, then egg, then crushed corn flakes.
2. Air fry at 385°F for 16 minutes, flipping halfway, until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
3. In a pan, combine light butter, minced garlic, soy sauce, brown sugar, and honey with ¼ cup water. Reduce until thickened.
4. Mix light sour cream with sriracha for the sauce.
5. Combine cucumber, red onion, avocado oil, hot sauce, salt, pepper, grated garlic, parsley, and red pepper flakes; toss together.
6. Assemble on ciabatta: chicken coated in the soy honey sauce, sriracha sour cream, and the cucumber mixture.

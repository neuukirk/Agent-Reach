# Creamy Jalapeño Sauce (Taco Bell Copycat)

**Source:** [@dishwithdrew on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DSvapxqEgs9/)

## Ingredients
- 1 (12 oz) jar pickled jalapeño slices with liquid (Mt. Olive brand)
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ to ½ tsp cayenne pepper
- ½ cup mayonnaise (Duke's preferred)
- ½ cup sour cream

## Instructions
1. **Blend jalapeños:** Add the entire jar of pickled jalapeños with liquid to a blender. Blend until completely smooth — a thin, pale green puree with no visible chunks.
2. **Mix base:** Combine mayo, sour cream, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, chili powder, cayenne, and salt in a bowl. Stir until smooth.
3. **Combine:** Add 7 tablespoons of the blended jalapeño puree to the mixture. Mix thoroughly. Taste and add more puree gradually to avoid excessive vinegar or heat.
4. **Chill:** Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate at least 1 hour before serving. Keeps refrigerated up to 4 days.

# Ultimate Slow-Cooked Pulled Beef

**Source:** [@joandbart_prep on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXZpkjeDO_V/)

**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients
- 2.5 lb (1.2 kg) beef joint
- 2 onions, sliced
- 2 carrots
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp rosemary
- 1 beef stock pot
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 4 tbsp gravy powder
- ¾ cup (200 ml) water
- Pinch of salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Add all ingredients except the gravy powder to a slow cooker; stir thoroughly.
2. Cook on low for 7 hours, then add the gravy granules. Continue cooking 1 hour on low until the beef shreds easily, then break it apart.
3. Return the shredded beef to the gravy and mix well. Place a tea towel between the pot and lid to absorb excess moisture, and cook a final hour on low. Serve with mashed potatoes and gravy.

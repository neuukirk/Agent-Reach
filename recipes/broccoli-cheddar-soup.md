# Broccoli Cheddar Soup

**Inspired by:** [@cookingwithkatiecross on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWpOhmEjw3B/) (reel was DM-only — "comment 'recipe'" — with no written recipe)
**Recipe source:** [Budget Bytes](https://www.budgetbytes.com/easy-broccoli-cheddar-soup/) — a web stand-in for the same dish.

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients
- 1 yellow onion
- 1 lb broccoli (before trimming stalk)
- 2 carrots
- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups whole milk
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
- 6 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded

## Instructions
1. Dice the onion and chop the broccoli into small pieces; peel and slice the carrots.
2. Sauté the butter and onion in a large pot over medium heat until soft and translucent.
3. Add the flour and sauté 2 minutes until lightly golden.
4. Pour in the chicken broth and whisk to dissolve the flour; bring to a simmer over medium-high, stirring often.
5. Reduce to medium; whisk in the milk and seasonings (paprika, garlic powder, pepper, cayenne).
6. Add the broccoli and carrots; simmer 15 minutes until the carrots are tender.
7. Lower to medium-low; stir in the cheese one handful at a time until melted.
8. Taste, add salt as needed, and serve.

# Sausage Gravy Biscuit Casserole

**Source:** [@g_bque on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DMId7_pgNJT/)

## Ingredients
- 1 lb hot sausage
- ⅓ cup flour
- 3½ cups milk
- ½ tsp Lawry's seasoned salt
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 1 can refrigerated biscuits

## Instructions
1. Brown the sausage in a pan over medium-high heat until fully cooked.
2. Add the flour to the cooked meat and stir for about 1 minute to cook out the raw flour taste.
3. Pour in the milk while stirring continuously until the gravy reaches the desired thickness.
4. Season with Lawry's and black pepper; mix thoroughly.
5. Cut each biscuit into quarters and distribute over the gravy.
6. Bake according to the biscuit package directions (~40 minutes per the creator).
7. Remove from oven, serve, and enjoy.

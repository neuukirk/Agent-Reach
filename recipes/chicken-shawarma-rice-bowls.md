# High-Protein Chicken Shawarma Rice Bowls

**Source:** [@jalalsamfit on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C7Pb5LfoBta/)

**Nutrition (per serving, makes 4):** 488 cal | 45 g protein | 42 g carbs | 14 g fat

## Ingredients

### Shawarma Chicken
- 750 g chicken breast, thinly sliced
- 4–5 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp dried parsley
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1.5 tsp salt & pepper
- 2 tsp cumin
- ¾ lemon, juiced
- 30–40 g light butter

### Spicy Samurai Sauce
- 200 g low-fat Greek yogurt
- 100 g light mayo
- 25–30 g hot sauce
- ½ tsp each: salt, pepper, garlic, onion powder, paprika
- ¼ lemon, juiced

### Cucumber Yogurt Mix
- 100 g Greek yogurt
- Chopped cucumbers & tomatoes
- Salt, garlic, cumin (to taste)
- Fresh parsley

### Golden Rice
- 200 g uncooked basmati rice (≈560 g cooked)
- 1 tsp each: turmeric, paprika, salt, cumin

## Instructions
1. Cook the basmati rice with the spices until done.
2. Coat the chicken slices thoroughly with the seasoning mixture on both sides.
3. Cook the chicken over medium heat using cooking spray or olive oil.
4. Make the samurai sauce by mixing yogurt, mayo, hot sauce, and seasonings.
5. Combine yogurt, cucumbers, tomatoes, and herbs for the cucumber yogurt mix.
6. Assemble bowls with rice, chicken, and both sauce components.

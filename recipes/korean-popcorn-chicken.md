# Korean Popcorn Chicken

**Source:** [@foodeanie on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZm8KwDItgK/)

> Quantities are for one medium chicken breast — scale up as needed.

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast
- 2 eggs
- Cornflakes
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp chilli powder
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp ground ginger

### Sauce
- 1.5 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice wine vinegar
- 2 tsp mirin
- 3 tbsp honey

## Instructions
1. Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces.
2. Coat the chicken with the listed seasonings.
3. Dip the seasoned chicken in egg, then immediately coat in crushed cornflakes.
4. Air fry at 200°C for 16 minutes, flipping halfway.
5. While cooking, combine all the sauce ingredients.
6. Once the chicken is done, heat and slightly reduce the sauce in a pan.
7. Add the cooked chicken and coat thoroughly.
8. Garnish with sesame seeds and spring onion, and serve.

# BBQ Cheeseburger Sliders

**Source:** [@headcountrybbq on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXSTxeLAUgz/)

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- Burger seasoning
- Hickory smoke BBQ sauce
- ½ large onion, sliced
- Slider buns or rolls
- 6 slices cheddar cheese
- 4 oz shredded Monterey Jack cheese
- 4–5 slices bacon, chopped
- ¼ stick butter

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Cook the ground beef over medium-high heat; season with burger seasoning. Remove from the pan, leaving the drippings.
3. Add the sliced onion to the pan, scraping up the fond. Cook until browned.
4. Return the beef to the pan, add BBQ sauce generously, and mix well.
5. Slice the slider buns. Layer with cheddar, the burger mixture, shredded cheese, bacon, and more BBQ sauce.
6. Top with the bun halves, brush with butter, and sprinkle burger seasoning on top.
7. Bake 10–12 minutes until heated through.
